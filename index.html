Tax Centre

When is everything due, and what do I need?

Key CRA dates for the 2026 tax year and a checklist to gather your documents.

Key CRA deadlines

  1. Dec 15, 2026Fourth quarterly instalment for individuals who pay by instalments
  2. Mar 1, 2027RRSP contribution deadline for 2026. T4, T4A and T5 slips due to employees and the CRA
  3. Mar 31, 2027T3 trust returns for trusts with a December 31 year end
  4. Apr 30, 2027Personal tax returns due, and any balance owing must be paid, including for the self-employed
  5. Jun 15, 2027Filing deadline for self-employed individuals and their spouses
  6. Jun 30, 2027T2 corporate return due for corporations with a December 31 year end

General information only. When a date falls on a weekend or holiday, the CRA treats a return as on time if filed the next business day. Confirm dates on canada.ca.
